Aluminum is a favorite for South Florida because it does not rust, it is strong for its weight, and its powder-coated finish stays attractive with minimal care. Our aluminum railings are available in a range of colors and styles, including picket, horizontal bar, and cable-inspired designs. Glass Railings That Preserve Your View:

Many West Palm Beach properties sit near water, golf courses, or skyline views worth showing off. Glass lets you do that. Tempered glass panels create a safe barrier that is almost invisible, allowing light to pass through and making spaces feel larger. Helpful Tips for Choosing and Caring for Your Railings

Match the Material to the Location:

Properties close to the water face more salt exposure, so corrosion resistance should be a top priority. Aluminum with a quality powder coat is an excellent choice for these conditions. Glass works especially well where views matter. Many owners combine both, using aluminum posts and rails with glass infill for strength and openness.

Plan for Code and Safety:

Railing height, spacing between components, and load requirements are regulated for good reason. When planning a project, ask your installer how the design meets local code and what documentation is available. A professional team will be able to answer these questions clearly.

Think About Maintenance Early:

One of the biggest advantages of aluminum and glass is how little care they need. Rinse aluminum with fresh water and mild soap every few months, especially near the coast, to remove salt and dirt. Clean glass with a non-abrasive glass cleaner and a soft cloth. Check fasteners and seals once a year, and call a professional if you notice looseness, damage, or signs of water intrusion.

Consider the Whole Property:

Railings are most effective when they work with the rest of the property. Consider window frames, door hardware, deck finishes, and exterior colors when choosing a style. A coordinated look raises curb appeal and can add value if you plan to sell. Our team is happy to offer suggestions based on what we have seen work well in similar homes and buildings.

Get a Written Estimate:

Before any work begins, ask for a clear written estimate that lists materials, finishes, and scope. This protects both sides and avoids surprises later. At MR Modern Aluminum & Glass Railings, we provide straightforward estimates so you can compare options with confidence.
